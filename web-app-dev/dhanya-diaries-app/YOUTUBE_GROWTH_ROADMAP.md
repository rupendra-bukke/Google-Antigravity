# Dhanya Diaries — YouTube Growth Roadmap

**Channel:** [@dhanyadiaries](https://www.youtube.com/@dhanyadiaries)  
**Goal:** Monetization + steady audience growth  
**Approach:** Fix one layer at a time. Do not try to change all 1,238 videos at once.

---

1. Open **Cursor** on Mac Mini → open this repo (or copy the markdown files).
2. Log into **YouTube Studio** in Chrome: https://studio.youtube.com

4. Agent uses **browser/computer** to edit Studio, or you paste from `YOUTUBE_STEP1_CHANNEL_COPY.md`.

**Important:** YouTube has no direct API login from cloud. Changes in Studio require your Mac session or manual paste.

## Phase 3 — Fix video metadata (Week 2)

**Fix:** Only high-impact videos first (not all 1,238).

### Priority order

1. **Last 20 uploads** — titles + descriptions (see `YOUTUBE_TITLE_FIXES.md`)
2. **Top 25 performers** — protect titles; only improve description + end screen
3. **Videos with misleading titles** — e.g. reposted celebrity clips (review for removal/unlist)

### Title formula

```
[Hook in Telugu or bilingual] | [Specific topic] | Dhanya Diaries
```

Examples:

- `నా Sunday Morning Routine ☀️ | Cooking + Cleaning | Dhanya Diaries`
- `Kids Lunchbox Day 12 🎒 | Quick Tiffin Recipe | Telugu Mom`
- `4AM Hoskote Biryani Review 🍗 | Bangalore Food | Dhanya Diaries`

### Description template (every new video)

```
[2 lines — what viewer will learn/feel]

🔔 Subscribe for weekly routines & recipes: https://www.youtube.com/@dhanyadiaries
📸 Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/dhanya.diaries

#DhanyaDiaries #TeluguVlogs #MorningRoutine #TeluguRecipes

---
About Dhanya Diaries: Telugu homemaker vlogs — real routines, easy cooking, and family life in Bangalore.
```

### Hashtags

- **3–5 in title** max
- **5–8 in description**
- Stop using `#viral` `#trending` on every video

## Phase 4 — Stop what hurts growth (Week 2–3)

| Stop | Replace with |
|------|----------------|
| Live streams (for now) | Pre-recorded Shorts |
| Weekly 15-min routine vlogs | 1 compiled monthly “Week in My Life” (10–12 min) |
| Same topic as Short + long-form same week | Short first; long-form only if Short gets 10K+ views |
| Uploading 10+ Shorts/week with low quality | 5 strong Shorts/week |
| `#viral` spam titles | Searchable Telugu + English titles |

## Phase 5 — Shorts system (Week 3–8)

**Target:** 5 Shorts per week, same series so subscribers return.

### Weekly calendar

| Day | Content |
|-----|---------|
| Monday | Morning routine Short |
| Tuesday | Recipe Short (60 sec) |
| Wednesday | Lunchbox series (numbered) |
| Thursday | Relatable homemaker moment |
| Saturday | Special — pooja / food review / family |

### Series to continue

- **Lunchbox Day 11, 12, 13…** (previous days got 30K–55K views)
- **Morning routine** variants (school day / holiday / Sunday)
- **One recipe per week** filmed as Short first

### Short checklist before publish

- [ ] Hook in first 2 seconds (action or question)
- [ ] Telugu voiceover or text overlay
- [ ] Face visible in thumbnail
- [ ] Title searchable (not just hashtags)
- [ ] Added to correct playlist

## Phase 6 — Long-form for watch hours (Week 4–12)

**Target:** 2 long videos per month, 10–15 minutes, high retention.

### Monthly long-form ideas

| Month | Video 1 | Video 2 |
|-------|---------|---------|
| 1 | Full Sunday routine (compile best Shorts + new footage) | 5 lunchbox recipes in one video |
| 2 | Bangalore breakfast food tour (3 spots) | Kitchen deep clean + organize |
| 3 | Sapthashanivara vratham — full procedure + prasadam | Village / family visit vlog |

**Goal per long-form video:** 2,000+ views × 12 min ≈ 400 watch hours each.

## Phase 7 — Monetization (when ready)

### Checklist before Apply

- [ ] Studio → **Earn** → confirm numbers
- [ ] **1,000+** subscribers
- [ ] **4,000** watch hours (12 months) **OR** **10M** Shorts views (90 days)
- [ ] **2-Step Verification** on Google account
- [ ] **AdSense for YouTube** linked (create in Studio, not adsense.google.com separately)
- [ ] No active **Community Guidelines** strikes
- [ ] Channel in **eligible country**

### If close on watch hours

- Publish 2 strong long-form videos this month
- Promote them in Shorts end screen: “Full recipe on my channel”
- Add **chapters** in long videos (improves retention)

### Early access tier (500 subs)

If full ads not ready, apply for **memberships / Super Thanks** at 500 subs + 3K hours or 3M Shorts views in 90 days.
